New controls

Mitigating risk

Many of the core risks existed in both manual and automated triage. We added specific controls for the new risks introduced by automation.

Mitigation

Auto route only when confident

  • Launch scope stayed limited to the supported categories with the strongest training data
  • Receiving teams follow current Departmental Triage practices for misrouted tickets
  • Recurring patterns are reported and are being analyzed to improve the model
Guardrail

The defined DUO scenario stayed narrow

  • Only eligible Duo requests triggered the automated response
  • Only missing-information cases were included
  • Halo sent approved canned text, not AI-generated free-form advice
Guardrail

Ready for rapid response

  • The Service Desk followed a documented manual recovery playbook to recover stalled tickets and make sure they were triaged
Guardrail

Performance was proven before routing

  • Silent production monitoring compared AI and agent decisions
  • Active routing followed a readiness review
  • Low-confidence and out-of-scope tickets stayed in the human process

How we rolled out

We tested in QA and production, set readiness gates, and moved only when the evidence supported it

2026

Build, test, prepare

Completed technical validation, UAT, guidance, training, communications, and operational readiness assessments

Aug 14–28

Monitoring phase began

Analyzed live tickets and recorded decisions without updating, assigning, or routing them

Aug 30

Readiness gate

Reviewed accuracy, exceptions, operations and people readiness before active routing

Aug 31

Active routing began

Eligible, high-confidence tickets began moving automatically. All others followed manual triage

Now

Hypercare and refinement

Continue monitoring results, recovering exceptions and tuning based on evidence.

Applying what we've learned

Automation
VS
Ambiguity

The MVP worked best where service patterns were clear. Working through our ambiguities is our next step toward improving auto triage

01
Clearer patterns

Auto Triage is trained against existing human patterns. We expected, and proved, that it inherited some misrouting habits from our original data set. Giving our staff the tools and resources to perform more accurately today, will improve our future data for even more accurate performance

02
Stronger service boundaries

When multiple IT service desks offer similar support, classification and model training need more context supported by clearer service definitions

03
Factoring entity info

Our next step is in improving how we consider the requestor's entity information. Improving matching to the requestor's local service desk when appropriate

04
Scaling thoughtfully

The next step is tightening accuracy of the current in-scope categories and then slowly expanding out to our next batch of high priority services
